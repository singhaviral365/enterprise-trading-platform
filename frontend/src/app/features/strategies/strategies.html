How your money is protected
  • Every strategy has a spending cap and a largest holding. They are worked out for you unless you set your own in More options, and a strategy that would break one does not trade.
  • Each order goes through the same order route as one you place yourself.
  • Switching a strategy off or cancelling it stops any order that has not been placed yet. It takes effect immediately.
  • A strategy only trades when the price moves through its level, never the moment you create it.
  • You are notified of every order it places, and when one is stopped or expires. You can turn these notices off in Settings.
